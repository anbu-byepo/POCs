1Ninto Analytics — Global Hub

Eight mobile screens in the Ninto app language (deep-green chrome, green primary action, light-green tags, warm grey media cards). Numbers in the labels map to your brief. The two parallel tracks (1, 2) and the link bridge (3) all surface on one post-detail screen, since a health professional thinks in posts, not in data sources — flagged as an assumption worth confirming. Traffic Ninto sends the other way — care seekers clicking through to the post at source — is carried as a green ↗ number on the roll-up, on each connected platform, on every synced post row, in the post detail, and on the link screen. Period selector, post filters and the ranking sort are live.
