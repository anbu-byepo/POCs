THINGS TO TRY

  1. Book and pay (patient): pick a doctor, a slot, Pay now. Try a decline, closing Checkout, and (demo controls) losing the success callback to see the re-check confirm or refund.
  2. Book and pay (clinic): Schedule → Calendar → tap a free slot → walk-in or scheduled → Cash, UPI, Card, payment link or pay at visit. A link shows up in the patient app to pay.
  3. Late patient: load the “Late patient example” scenario, press +5 → at 10:30 Arjun is Reserved with a 10-minute grace. Call next in the clinic, then check in from the patient app within or after grace.
  4. Getting there: open today's appointment → Plan my trip. Every way to get there (walk, two-wheeler, auto, cab, car, bus/metro) shows time, arrival and leave-by time, and an itemised cost breakup, with a best pick and a Google Maps route. Times come from the Google Maps Routes API when GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY is set on the server, otherwise estimates. With location off, pick a demo start (e.g. Adyar). Tap I'm leaving now and watch the clinic's Up next panel.
  5. Dynamic queue: load “Late patient example”. Arjun said on the call he's 20 min away by auto, so Up next already places him by that arrival. Run the clock past his grace: he isn't sent to the end. He goes in as if booked for (arrival − grace), ahead of later slots.
  6. Voice engine switch: Clinic → Settings → Arrival calls → Voice AI engine: Scripted, Claude + browser speech, or ElevenLabs + Claude (in the patient app, or a real phone call through a SIP trunk / Twilio). See Costing for what each costs at your volume.
  7. AI arrival call: 15 minutes before a slot, un-arrived patients without live location are called, with one follow-up if they're 10+ min past the time they gave. With ANTHROPIC_API_KEY on the server it's a live Claude conversation (spoken aloud; reply by mic, tap or typing). Otherwise it's scripted. The clinic row shows coming or not, ETA, travel mode, a summary and any note for the doctor.
  8. Refunds: cancel as the patient (rule depends on hours left) or press Doctor cancels (full refund). Online payments are refunded through Razorpay.
