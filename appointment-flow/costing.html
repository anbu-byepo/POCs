Arrival calls: IVR vs Claude / Gemini vs ElevenLabs vs Gemini Live

The same calls at your volume, on each engine. "Per call" is one call of the average length you set (the IVR uses its own, shorter length). In the text-model options, the AI model you pick (Claude or Gemini) is the brain. ElevenLabs adds the voice, the hearing and places the call. Gemini Live hears, thinks and speaks in one model, so it needs no ElevenLabs, but real calls need your own small server to carry the call audio between the phone line and Gemini. The IVR has no AI: a keypad menu over the phone line.

Real phone calls: by phone line

Each real-call engine on each Indian phone line, at your volume. Exotel and Plivo both stream live call audio to a server (Exotel's Voicebot applet, Plivo Audio Streams), which is what Gemini Live needs; ElevenLabs connects to them over SIP. Exotel's per-minute rate isn't published, so its figures are an estimate until you have a quote.

Exotel's monthly figures leave out its plan or bundle fee and number rental, which Exotel quotes (bundles from ₹9,999), so it looks cheaper per month than it is. Plivo's include ₹200/month per number; Twilio's $1.15. Gemini Live rows include the call server.
