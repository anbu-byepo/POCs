Sources and assumptions

  • Google Maps, India pricing: pricing-india, SKU triggers: sku-details/routes
  • ElevenLabs Agents: pricing/agents, LLM billing: docs/llm, Tamil: text-to-speech and speech-to-text
  • Claude API: platform.claude.com pricing
  • Phone lines: Plivo India, Twilio India and its India rules, Exotel (no published per-minute rate; the figure used is a third-party estimate)
  • Razorpay: razorpay.com/pricing
  • IVR prompts: Twilio text-to-speech rates, Plivo pricing mirror, India IVR rate range (vendor guide)
  • Hosting (servers for the apps and API endpoints) isn't included here.
  • Claude tokens per call are our estimate for this script: about 5 turns, ~1,400 input and ~250 output tokens per turn (low effort), so ~7,000 in and ~1,250 out per one-minute call. Real calls vary; ElevenLabs reports each call's actual cost, and the clinic row shows it.
  • Rupee figures use the exchange rate you set above. All prices exclude GST unless stated; add 18% GST where your provider charges it.
