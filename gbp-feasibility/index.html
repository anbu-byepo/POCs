Ninto -- Google Business Profile (feasibility spike, NINTO-550)

Real app, not a mockup -- tap Continue with Google, pick a Google account in the real consent popup, and work with that account's real Business Profile listings. Screens follow the "1A -- Google Business Profile -- setup & management" design. No backend -- everything happens in this browser tab. See ../audit/gbp-feasibility-plan.md. All POCs

Feasibility spike setup (not part of the design -- dev only)

Defaults to the same Web application client as the YouTube spike (hardcoded in app.js, not a secret). Its Cloud project must be approved for the Business Profile APIs (quota must read 300 QPM, not 0). An override here is saved to this browser's localStorage. Its Authorized JavaScript origins must list every origin this page is opened from (http://localhost:8000, and the deployed origin with no path), and its consent screen must list .../auth/business.manage and .../auth/userinfo.email, with your account added as a test user.

On: edits and creates are sent with validateOnly=true (Google validates the payload, nothing changes), and writes that have no dry-run form -- claim, verify, enter code, invite or remove a manager -- are skipped and logged. Turn off only against a test listing, never a real HWP's live listing.
