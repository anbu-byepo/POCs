Ninto -- Connect & Publish (YouTube)

Real app, YouTube only, for now. Click Connect, pick any Google account in the real consent popup, see that channel's real videos, and upload a real video file straight to it. No backend -- everything happens in this browser tab, authenticated with your own OAuth access token.

Saved to this browser's localStorage only. Must be a Web application-type client with http://localhost:8000 (and/or http://127.0.0.1:8000) under Authorized JavaScript origins, and its OAuth consent screen must list both .../auth/youtube.readonly and .../auth/youtube.upload as scopes (Testing status is fine as long as your account is added as a test user).
